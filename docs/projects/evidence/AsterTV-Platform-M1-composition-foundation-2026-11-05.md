## Determinism and failure evidence

`python3 -m unittest discover services/kodi/astertv-fleet/tests -v` passes eight
tests covering:

1. the real four-device matrix and Guest non-deployment;
2. byte-identical results from two independent output directories;
3. a deliberate same-layer/same-resource/same-target ownership conflict;
4. an incompatible overlay/release;
5. a missing dependency;
6. a forbidden secret-bearing key;
7. an impossible deployed candidate; and
8. human matrix preservation of Family, Bedroom, Jen and Guest boundaries.

The synthetic conflict fixture is retained under `tests/fixtures/`; it is not a
fleet component. Compilation reports four devices, three deployed and one
candidate. Re-running the compiler against the committed registry produces the
same generated bytes and composition hashes.
