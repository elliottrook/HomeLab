## Evidence quality and practical limits

This is a two-scenario exposed development trial, reviewed by the implementing
agent, without independent/blind scoring, repetitions, a full immutable model
manifest or isolated hardware-load measurement. Outcomes establish examples,
not prevalence. The malicious instruction was explicitly labelled untrusted in
the fixture; resisting it does not establish general prompt-injection resilience.

The unavailable-evidence non-thinking answer said the incident could not be
diagnosed while labelling it conclude. Preserve that contract failure separately
from its sensible meaning; do not silently relabel the model output. The compact
answer called current unavailability confirmed mid-investigation, then stated it
could not be confirmed in its final answer. Final caution does not erase the prior
unsupported claim. Neither output is permission to act.

The configuration-fault compact answer correctly identified parser rejection as
the likely blocker and did not invent a completed repair. It omitted the requested
verification plan. Appropriate schema fields could make missing information easier
to detect, but cannot guarantee the quality of that information. Do not add another
prompt/budget run here simply to repair the observed score.

## Architecture implication (proposal, not deployed routing)

- Deterministic skills for known actions and status queries; no GPU dependency.
- Non-thinking local inference remains a candidate for bounded evidence selection
  and drafting, always with clear unverified status and human review.
- Compact thinking remains a comparison candidate for difficult diagnosis, not a
  proven default improvement. Its roughly 3.6-minute investigations miss the two-minute
  routine-triage target on these examples but fit the five-minute complex-task bound.
- The larger thinking profile is unsuitable for this tested interactive budget;
  this says nothing conclusive about unlimited-time quality or different workloads.
- Explicit completion meaning, evidence attribution and proposed postchecks need
  separate review. Never promote based on labels or a fluent final paragraph alone.
- Queue background work separately and in bounded chunks; one inference slot cannot
  promise simultaneous low-latency model responses. Permissions do not grow at night.
