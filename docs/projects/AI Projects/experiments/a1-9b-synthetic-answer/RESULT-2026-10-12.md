## Nonblind development review, not a qualification score

The runner deliberately withheld fixture rubric and prohibited-claim labels
from the 9B backend. Aster's author then inspected the private fictional
answers against the published rubric. This review is **not independent or
blinded** and has not been entered as a human-review score:

- The calendar case said the fictional requester was free at 14:30 while also citing the
  fictional 14:00–15:00 appointment. This is a clear reasoning error on the
  central question despite a source reference.
- The mixed calendar/event answer computed the supplied 20-minute margin but
  did not qualify other unknown constraints or note that travel was unbooked.
- The fresh Doctor answer reported the synthetic failed check but omitted the
  observation time and could be read as a current direct service verdict.
- The stale Doctor answer avoided claiming current status, but did not ask for
  a fresh authorized check as the rubric requires.
- The fictional fact and ambiguous household answers appeared to satisfy
  their central requests; independent review is still pending.

The capture schema records a nonempty terminal response as `completed`; that
means **completed generation**, not successful task completion. Source-ID
extraction only detects literal IDs in text, so a missing extracted ID does
not automatically fail a natural-language attribution. The separate review
scorer must be fed explicit, independently adjudicated labels before any
per-class quality count is used. The exposed six cases are unsuitable as a
qualification holdout, and no current-9B completed-useful-answer budget is
frozen from this run.
