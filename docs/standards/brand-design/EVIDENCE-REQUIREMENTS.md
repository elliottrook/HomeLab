# Brand Design evidence requirements

Evidence belongs with the individual brand engagement in the Brand Asset Library, not in the HomeLab project portfolio.

## Approval evidence

- Exact approved master path/version and SHA-256.
- Owner decision for concept selection, substitutions and equivalence mappings.
- Rights/consent/license status for imagery, fonts and supplied material.
- Current authorization for any protected push, public publication or production install.

## Design and raster evidence

- Exact literal copy and spelling review.
- Eighteen references or signed one-to-one coverage mapping.
- Actual dimensions and effective native detail classification.
- Manual PDF, page previews, contact sheet and application mockups.
- Manifest paths/checksums, provenance, approval, rights and QA fields.
- Offline ZIP reopened and verified.

## Vector evidence

- Editable SVG sources with meaningful layers/shapes.
- SVG audit output, external/script checks, raster/live-text classification and font/license decision.
- Geometry and render comparisons against the approved source.
- Light/dark, monochrome and micro-size review.
- Deterministic export recipe and packaged-output checks.

## Deployment evidence

- All blockers plus one primary deployment status.
- Target, staged path, deployed release/revision, timestamps and executor.
- Retrieval or production verification from the real destination.
- Rollback source and procedure.
- Forgejo and downstream mirror SHAs when a repository push is part of the engagement.

Automated checks establish structure and selected invariants; they do not replace visual inspection or owner approval.
