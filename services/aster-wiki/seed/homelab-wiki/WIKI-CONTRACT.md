# Human Wiki Contract

1. Reviewed live reports, domain systems of record and `homelab-reference`
   outrank this repository for current operational facts.
2. Operator-authored pages declare authority and review state. Imported
   upstream material is versioned reference material only.
3. Every import records source identity, version, hashes, retrieval time,
   extraction method, locators and licensing decision.
4. Generated content cannot overwrite authored paths. Unknown licensing,
   secrets, unsafe types, expanded scope or incomplete extraction quarantines.
5. The Aster mirror is reproducible and non-authoritative. Corrections happen
   here or at the adopted source, never by editing generated mirror entries.
6. No credentials, private keys, tokens or passwords belong in the corpus.
