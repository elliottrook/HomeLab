# Sample content — never publish

Every asset and story in the accepted Sites baseline is treated as demonstration
content. Some photographs are web-sourced previews and others have incomplete
production provenance. None is launch-approved.

The private Content Desk must display `SAMPLE — NOT FOR PUBLICATION` beside
these records. The content model sets `sample: true`, which is an unconditional
approval blocker even when other metadata appears complete. A launch record
must be created deliberately from a selected Immich asset and must pass rights,
consent, alt-text and immutable-version checks independently.
