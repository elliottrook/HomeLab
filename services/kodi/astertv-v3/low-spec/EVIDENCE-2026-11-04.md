Jellyfin and YouTube code is installed but both add-ons are disabled. Their
first-run dialogs attempted account/device authorization; those flows were
cancelled, their incomplete `addon_data` removed, and no production identity
was cloned. They require dedicated low-spec identities or deliberate owner
sign-in. Seerr/request-broker parity requires a dedicated source identity and
firewall decision. Aster AI parity is not available: M3 still requires a
search-only broker identity and contract and expressly denies general TV
access in the meantime. No model workload was run, so no Lab Hours reservation
was required.

## Verdict

The M93p is a viable low-spec AsterTV client for the accepted v2 UI and 1080p
H.264 media. It is not a production-equivalent 4K client because Haswell lacks
HEVC/VP9/AV1 hardware decode, and it is not yet a production-equivalent service
client because M2/M3 identity work, request-broker access and the screensaver
defect remain open. Do not promote it as an identical production model until
those project gates are satisfied.
