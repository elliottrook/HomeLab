The seven navigation WAV files in this add-on are transformations of Kenney's
recorded `mouseclick1` sample from the CC0 "51 UI sound effects" pack:
https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks

`startup.wav` is an original Aster soundscape produced for this project.

The Aster icon remains subject to the separately recorded private Aster
brand-family terms.
